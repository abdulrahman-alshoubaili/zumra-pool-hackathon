The problem

For young professionals, first-time investors and salaried savers with BD 10 to 500 to spare, investing feels out of reach.

Limited capital

Many products need large minimum amounts.

Hard to find the right fit

Options are complex and scattered across platforms.

Unclear risks and fees

People hesitate when they can't see them.

The solution

A feature inside the BisB app where people pool small amounts into one managed, Sharia-compliant portfolio.

Zumra Stable

Lower risk, steadier returns.

Zumra Balanced

A mix of risk levels.

Zumra Growth

Higher growth, higher risk.

How it works

From first deposit to withdrawal in five steps.

InvestJoin in the BisB app from BD 10 and add monthly.
PoolFunds go to a segregated pool, not a person.
ManageA licensed manager invests per portfolio policy.
EarnProfit is shared pro-rata, net of fees.
ExitRequest any time, paid within 14 days.

Sharia structure & business model

Built on Mudarabah: investors provide the capital (Rab al-Mal) and BisB provides the expertise (Mudarib). Funds go into Sukuk, Murabaha, Ijarah and compliant equities, with no guaranteed return and Sharia board approval.

1%

Annual management fee on assets under management.

2%

Share of investment profit under the Mudarabah ratio.

BD 10

Minimum to start, with monthly auto top-ups.

14 days

Exit window, funded by a cash and short-term Murabaha buffer.
